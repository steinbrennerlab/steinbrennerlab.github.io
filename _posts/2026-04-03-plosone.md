Brian's paper on natural variation in INR is out in PLOS ONE <a href="https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0343332">here</a>! Some common bean landraces are insensitive to the caterpillar peptide In11. Brian, Wes, Tonio, and Ava found that this variation tracks with the expression level of <i>INR</i>, linked to promoter variation, rather than with changes in the receptor's coding sequence. Congrats Brian and team!
